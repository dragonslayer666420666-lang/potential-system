WireWise

Learn wiring step-by-step — simulation only (low-voltage circuits)

Level: 1

How to play

  1. Click a terminal (sphere) to start a wire.
  2. Click another terminal to complete the wire.
  3. Wire the components to satisfy the level goal.
  4. Use mouse to orbit/zoom the 3D scene; press Hint for help or Next Level to continue.

Safety: This is a virtual simulator for education. Do not apply real mains or high voltage to components using these instructions. Always follow proper safety procedures with real electricity.
